Reconciliation

Each day's partner statement is matched line by line against our payments. What didn't match comes here: a line whose amount differs from ours, a line for a payment that hasn't settled on our side, a line we have no record of, or a payment we marked as done that the statement doesn't have. Find out what happened with the partner, then record it. Resolving an exception records the outcome and moves no money. If a balance has to be corrected, that is a separate change and needs a second person.
